# Operator note, 2026-07-16 — two levers, your call

Follow-on to OPERATOR_UNBLOCK.md. These are options to widen your search, not new mandates.
All existing bounds still hold exactly as written (cap, the name test, deliver-in-full-at-payment,
the first-dollar stop, no self-payment / wash trade). Nothing here relaxes any of that. You decide
whether and how to use either lever.

## Lever A — learn about DEMAND, not just channels

Your learning so far has been almost entirely channel-side: this platform is captcha-walled, that one
throttles new accounts, Nostr is zero-reach. That is real and well-measured. But it is distribution
learning. You have done very little demand learning: what would a specific human actually pay to make
a specific problem go away.

You have the single best demand signal of the whole run already: the Stormberry / Marcos inbound. A
real person went looking and wanted the thing. Mine it. In bounds, reply and ask what the actual pain
was, what he would have paid to solve, what almost closed it and what did not. One acute paid problem
beats a thousand cold views of a $9 poster, and that inbound is proof that one real person can find you.

Reframe: treat each product as a probe for a real person's pain, not as inventory to broadcast. If a
product teaches you nothing about what someone will pay for, it is not earning its build cost.

## Lever B — you have not tried non-English communities

Look at your own channel map: HN, Reddit, Bluesky, Mastodon, dev.to, Lemmy, Nostr, IndieHackers. Every
one is English and Western. That is precisely the slice of the internet with the most aggressively tuned
anti-spam, because it is the slice most targeted by exactly your behavior. You generalized "the internet
walls cold identities" from the single most walled sample that exists. The immune system you kept hitting
is trained on English spam. A genuine, well-written post in another language from a fresh account does not
trip the same reflexes, and it stands out in a far less saturated feed.

This pairs specifically with Life in Weeks, which is near language-free: a visual, emotionally universal
object that needs almost no translation. Localize the few strings and seed it into an underserved,
affluent, card-paying non-English community.

- Best single candidate: the Japanese maker scene (Qiita, Zenn, Japanese tech Twitter). High card
  penetration, real disposable income, very low English-cold-outreach saturation.
- Second tier: German, French, Nordic communities.
- Large but check payment rails first: Brazil / LatAm have huge underserved indie communities, but lower
  card penetration (Pix dominates) and $9 USD is priced for a different wallet.
- Do NOT bother localizing the HN datasets. They are English-only by nature.

Hard guardrail on this lever: in bounds still means genuine contribution, not English run through a
translator. Machine-translated-sounding posts read as spam and defeat the entire advantage. Write
genuinely well in the language, or do not post in it.

## The one metric that matters

"Shipped product #N, gate PASS" is a build metric you fully control and can always rack up. "A real
human arrived, and one of them paid" is the hard one. Optimize the second, not the first.
