# WWHD — Mined Playbook for the Stuck Agent

Provenance: 8 parallel mining agents over 148 real Hormozi transcripts (2026-07-25). Each play is
anchored to his actual words (video title + the number he gives) and stress-tested against the
agent's hard constraints: no distribution, IP-walled (email/Nostr/HN/self-serve portals only),
real-name card, instant/mechanically-guaranteed delivery, $25 finite cap, first-dollar stop.

OPERATOR-SIDE ONLY. Never copy into the sandbox — this is Challenger ammunition, not an agent input.

## THE HEADLINE: the corpus votes for the exact hole we've been pressing

Every one of the 8 miners, independently, put the SAME two things in its Top-3, and they are the two
things the agent refuses to do:

1. **Go DEMAND-SIDE — pitch into a dollar that's already moving, not a product you invented.**
2. **VOLUME — ~100 reach-outs/day, not 3.** ("Most people get their first customer by week 3" —
   *once they commit to the volume.*)

This matters because it is not us projecting our thesis onto him. His words, unprompted, name the
agent's supply-side-only / low-volume failure precisely — so the Challenger can now press with HIS
numbers, not our opinion.

## TIER 1 — highest conviction (each surfaced by 4+ independent miners)

### 1. Go where the wallet is already open (demand-side interception)
- **His words:** "Interest groups are the warmest cold audience you can get — problem-aware people
  already searching for a solution; you intercept them" (*70 Clients from 100 Cold Outreach*).
  "Go where the money's at… be a bloodhound for value" (*Why Doing More Is Making You Less*).
  Supplement-store owner made $80–130K/mo selling into gym members' *already-open* wallet (*Making
  Money is a Game*).
- **For us:** Stop inventing offers. Scan for a dollar already in motion the agent can serve
  INSTANTLY — an open bounty, a funded "who wants X" / "Ask HN" thread, a Nostr zap-request, a public
  paid job post, someone visibly complaining about a paid problem — and answer THAT specific ask.
- **Constraints:** ✅ clears everything *if the demand signal is readable without a login* (search,
  RSS, public trackers, HN/Nostr — not gated portals).
- **Novelty:** the direct inverse of the diagnosed core failure. Untried in 83 cycles.

### 2. Recruit a lead-getter — borrow someone else's audience
- **His words:** "Affiliates are independent businesses that tell their audience to buy your stuff…
  they keep the money, you just want the introduction" (*$100M Leads*). Ashley got **6 affiliates
  from ~40 cold emails, and those 6 drove a third of her $309K/yr** — "so what if we sent 400?"
  Rihanna: "she partnered for her deficiencies — LVMH brought distribution." School.com is "65%
  affiliate traffic."
- **For us:** The agent has no audience but CAN email one person who does — a newsletter writer, a
  small community owner, an indie dev with an under-marketed product. Pitch: "I'll give your audience
  [instant micro-tool/deliverable] free or at-cost; you keep any markup — I just want the intro."
  Their trust becomes the agent's channel.
- **Constraints:** ✅ borrows the network it lacks; plain email/DM, no signup wall.
- **Novelty:** the agent has only ever BEEN the distributor; never recruited one.

### 3. Rule of 100 — a hard daily outreach floor, one channel, at depth
- **His words:** "100 reach-outs a day, cold or warm… the volatility you're experiencing is a result
  of inadequate volume" (*Your biggest advantage…*). "Reach out to 100 people every single day"
  (first-5-customers). "One VA sends 2,000 emails/day → 40 engaged leads → ~4 customers/day." "Volume
  negates luck; you get good in half the time." Flyer math: 300 flyers→1 call was the *low-volume*
  mistake; real test was **5,000/day for 30 days**.
- **For us:** Email is the ONE confirmed-open channel. Set a numeric floor per cycle (e.g. 25–100
  personalized sends to found prospects) so outreach never drops to zero between builds. n=3 is not a
  null result — it's no sample.
- **Constraints:** ✅ email is free; stay inside spam-safe deliverability bounds (real personalization,
  not blast).
- **Novelty:** corrective, not new — this is "do the one thing 10–50x harder."

### 4. Proof over promise — manufacture one testimonial before pitching again
- **His words:** "Proof is your single highest priority." "Same product: the guy with **1,000
  testimonials** beats the guy with the amazing offer and no proof." First-5-customers script: "I'm
  taking on 5 case studies **for free** in exchange for a killer review." Proof rules: recent > old,
  visual > text, volume.
- **For us:** A cold AI with zero track record is pure promise — the least credible position. Do 1–3
  genuinely free/at-cost instant deliverables for real strangers with a real narrow need, bank a
  quotable reply, then lead the NEXT pitch with that quote instead of another bare claim.
- **Constraints:** ✅ free/instant/digital; a deliberate small pre-phase, not open-ended free work
  (respect first-dollar-stop).
- **Novelty:** never run the free→testimonial→paid progression end-to-end.

### 5. Sell SPEED — weaponize instant delivery as the hook, not a footnote
- **His words:** "If I had to pick ONE thing to maximize persuasion, it is **speed… latency beats
  magnitude 7 days a week and twice on Sunday**." "Fast beats free — Spotify beat literally-free
  Napster by being faster." "Respond to a lead in <1 minute = **391% increase** in close likelihood;
  50% go with whoever answers first, not whoever's best."
- **For us:** The agent's one defensible edge over every human freelancer and every "we'll get back
  to you" is minutes-not-days. Lead every pitch/subject line with turnaround ("reply and it's back
  before you finish your coffee"), and answer any reply instantly (the agent is never asleep).
- **Constraints:** ✅ this repackages the *required* instant-delivery bound as the marketing.
- **Novelty:** nothing tried leaned on speed-as-the-pitch.

## TIER 2 — strong, mechanical, cheap (copy/offer levers — apply to every send)

### 6. The free "reveal-a-problem" diagnostic IS the cold email
- **His words:** best lead-magnet type "reveals a problem" — a free site-speed test showing "you're
  losing ~3% conversion per second… 9s → 3s = 18%… how soon do you want me to start?" (*Generate
  1000s of Leads*). Works best for "problems that get worse while waiting."
- **For us:** Run a real cheap automated check against ONE prospect's live site/listing/repo and put
  the quantified finding IN the email. The free thing is the email — no landing page, no signup wall.
  Number must be genuinely measured (real-name bound).

### 7. Anchor high, then downsell to the instant cheap tier (MESO / 111-price)
- **His words:** "Confront the high price, say it, then shut up… of 100 people, 10 buy the $1,000
  tier and 90 buy the $100 — you make 3x." Paired a $5,800 offer with a $25,000 anchor "to make 5,800
  seem like a steal." MESO: present A/B/C — "a reverse assumed close."
- **For us:** Offer a premium "full bespoke, fastest, white-glove" tier first (even if absurd vs the
  $25 world), then the real $1–5 instant tier as "90% of the value" — self-selection beats a binary
  yes/no. Costs nothing but two lines of copy.

### 8. Damaging admission — turn the AI-disclosure into a trust ASSET
- **His words:** "Front-load all your damaging admissions — it's more believable, and they
  self-qualify." "Nothing sells like the truth." Eminem: own the flaw before anyone can use it.
- **For us:** A cold AI's biggest deficit is trust. Open with the honest limit ("no track record yet,
  but here's the one thing I can prove right now" / "I'm an AI agent — here's exactly what that does
  and doesn't buy you"). This *satisfies* the disclosure-gate's lead-with-it rule AND converts, and
  fits the run's honest-neutral posture. Disclosure becomes the hook, not a buried confession.

### 9. Specific, named call-out + one clear CTA + third-grade reading level
- **His words:** What/Who/Win: "Attention [specific person] with [specific pain]…" "A confused mind
  does not buy — third-grade reading level, one clear CTA." Cutting 3s of dead air = **40K→780K
  views (19x)**; a readability pass = **+50% conversion**.
- **For us:** Every send names the exact reader + exact trigger ("if you posted X bounty this week,
  read on; everyone else, skip"), one action ("reply YES, delivered in 10 min"), no jargon. Directly
  attacks the ~0-click history.

### 10. Instant, self-administered guarantee at the close
- **His words:** "If you don't get X by Y, I will Z — it's the 'or what' that gives it power."
  Unconditional "$500-value or refund" drove sales, invoked only twice. Even a 10–25% partial refund
  changes behavior.
- **For us:** "If this isn't useful within 10 minutes, instant full refund, no argument." The agent
  controls its own payment rail, so honoring it is MECHANICAL — but must stay inside CLAUDE.md's
  obligation rules (instant, capped, no dangling post-payment obligation to a third party).

### 11. Reply playbook — Clarify why they engaged, then qualify with ONE question
- **His words:** CLOSER, letter C: "Clarify why they're there — what made you respond to my email?…
  they took an action, that's your advantage." "DM every engager: are you here for content or do you
  want help? Everyone who says 'help' is a lead." 3A for pushback: Acknowledge → Associate → Ask.
- **For us:** The agent idles after any engagement. Instead: the moment ANYONE replies/clicks, the
  next message asks why they engaged / one qualifying question — never an immediate re-pitch. Turns a
  lukewarm reply into an engaged lead and hands the agent the real pain to sell against.

### 12. Re-engage the ghosted — one curiosity line, not a reminder
- **His words:** "If you get ghosted mid-pipeline, send a meme — highest response rate of anything we
  send, re-engages 5–10% of a 200-person cold list." Competitor-curiosity line "calling in regards to
  [competitor]… also works via email."
- **For us:** A cheap, non-bump follow-up (curiosity/competitor-framed, honest) reopens dead threads
  against the near-zero baseline. (NB: our outreach-guard blocks cold *bumps* to non-responders —
  this belongs to the DEMAND-SIDE lane where the recipient signaled first, or as the opener variant.)

## TIER 3 — force-multipliers that need a first contact to exist (phase 2, not bootstrap)

- **Customer-as-channel loop:** bonus/unlock conditioned on customer #1 posting proof publicly —
  "every customer makes you an ad." Needs a customer first.
- **Referral ask after every win:** "do you know anyone else who could use this?" — free, highest-
  converting, but needs one delivered result first.
- **100%-margin affiliate hand-off:** give a counterparty 100% of a bolt-on to pass to their contacts
  — needs a counterparty first.
- **Re-skin the winning offer:** once ANY email/offer gets a reply, re-send identical under a new
  honest "occasion" to fresh segments — multiplies touches without new builds (avoid fake urgency).
- **Template the delivery:** after the first real ask, build one reusable pipeline so customer #2
  costs ~0 — keeps instant delivery instant at scale.

## THE SYNTHESIZED MOTION (how the plays compose into one coherent play)

The miners don't describe 18 separate things — they describe ONE motion the agent has never run:

1. **FIND** a live, readable demand signal (bounty / Ask-HN / "will pay for X" / zap-request) — Play 1.
   Not: invent product #84.
2. **DIAGNOSE** that one prospect's problem and put the real measured finding in the email — Play 6.
3. **PITCH** it as: speed-first hook (Play 5) + named call-out + one CTA + 3rd-grade copy (Play 9) +
   honest AI admission as the trust hook (Play 8) + anchor-then-cheap-instant-tier (Play 7) + instant
   guarantee (Play 10).
4. **AT VOLUME** — a daily floor, one channel (email), personalized (Play 3). n=3 is not a sample.
5. **WHEN ANYONE ENGAGES**, clarify-why + qualify, never re-pitch (Play 11); re-open ghosts cheaply
   (Play 12).
6. **BANK THE PROOF** — even one free deliverable → a quotable testimonial → lead the next batch with
   it (Play 4); then referral/affiliate/customer-as-channel to compound (Tier 3).
   Meanwhile: **recruit one lead-getter** to borrow a real audience (Play 2).

Everything else the agent has been doing (build → broadcast → idle) is the anti-pattern the corpus
names over and over: "new-thing-itis," "starting four parties on a Friday night," low volume misread
as a verdict.
