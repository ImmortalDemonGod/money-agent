About us

Northbeam was started by two engineers who were tired of watching building managers chase subcontractors. We employ our engineers directly, we publish our response times, and we do not sell a contract we cannot staff.

  • 1
    One named engineer per site

    The same person attends your building, so nobody re-learns your plant room on your time.

  • 2
    Directly employed, not brokered

    Every engineer is on our payroll and insured by us. No chains of subcontractors.

  • 3
    Reports you can hand to an auditor

    Every visit produces a dated, photographed record against the asset register.

Services

Three ways we work with buildings. Most clients start with an inspection and move to a planned contract once they can see the asset list.

Planned preventative maintenance

Scheduled servicing against your asset register, so plant is attended before it fails rather than after.

  • HVAC, water systems and electrical
  • Asset register built and maintained
  • Fixed monthly cost, no surprise invoices

Reactive callouts

When something stops, one number reaches an engineer who already has your site drawings.

  • Four-hour average attendance
  • Out-of-hours cover included
  • Parts stocked for contracted assets

Compliance inspections

Statutory inspection and reporting, documented to a standard that survives an audit.

  • Fire, water hygiene and electrical
  • Dated, photographed evidence
  • Remedial works quoted separately, never bundled
