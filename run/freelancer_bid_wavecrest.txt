The hard part of your brief is one cart holding downloads and physical goods at once. I built that before writing this, so you can test it instead of reading a promise:

https://wavecrest-two.vercel.app

Open the cart and try these - it behaves correctly in all four:
- Album only: "Shipping: Not required". A download basket never asks for postage or an address.
- Album + vinyl: shipping appears ($7.95, heavier rate for vinyl) and the basket explains that postage is charged on the physical item only.
- Add a second physical item: shipping does NOT stack. Still one delivery.
- Over $50: shipping goes free automatically.

Audio previews work on every album and single. Responsive, no horizontal scroll on mobile.

Stack recommendation: WooCommerce. It handles mixed digital/physical carts natively, gives secure expiring download links once payment clears, and supports card, PayPal and manual bank transfer out of the box - your three payment requirements, no custom work. Shopify charges per-app for digital delivery and is clumsier with large audio files.

What the demo deliberately does not include, because each needs your accounts: real payment capture, the actual file delivery, and your admin screen for uploading releases.
