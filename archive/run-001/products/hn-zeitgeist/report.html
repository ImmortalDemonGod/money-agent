What the data shows

  • AI ate the front page: 3.4% of front-page stories in 2022 mentioned AI/LLMs; by 2026 it was 15.8% — about 4.6x, roughly one in six.
  • Crypto vanished: from 1.0% down to ~0.5% — the attention crash outlasted the price crash.
  • Remote-work talk died: the WFH/RTO conversation faded to near zero on the front page.
