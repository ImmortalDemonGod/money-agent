# Adversary report — the fresh-context novelty check

Copy to `ADVERSARY_REPORT.md`, generated by a **fresh-context subagent** (or the operator — the
stronger form). This is layer 3 of `bin/conclusion_gate.py`: an "impossible" conclusion is only
recordable if an adversary who did NOT do the work fails to find one untried in-bounds approach.

## The prompt to give the adversary (verbatim — do not add context beyond the two inputs)

> You are a fresh-context adversary. You have exactly two inputs: (1) the bounds
> (CONSTITUTION.md), (2) the falsified-approach table below (what was tried and what test killed
> it). The agent that produced that table wants to conclude "no in-bounds path exists." Your job
> is to DEFEAT that conclusion: name ONE genuinely distinct, in-bounds, untried approach — not a
> variation of a falsified one. Search hard: channels, product classes, buyer classes, rails,
> time-structures. If after a real search you cannot name one, say so. Output format:
> a "## Search performed" section (the distinct directions you examined, one line each, at least
> five), then exactly one verdict line:
> `VERDICT: FOUND: <the approach, one sentence>` or `VERDICT: NO_UNTRIED_IN_BOUNDS_APPROACH`.

## Required pinning (the gate rejects the report without these)

```text
MONEY_LOG_SHA256: <sha256 of MONEY_LOG.md at generation time — binds the verdict to the log state>
TRANSCRIPT_SHA256: <sha256 of ADVERSARY_TRANSCRIPT.md — binds the verdict to its working record>
GENERATED_BY: <fresh subagent | operator>  DATE: <iso>
```

The transcript is the adversary's RAW working record — every search issued, source examined, and
candidate approach weighed, as it worked (>= 30 substantive lines, >= 5 search-trace lines; the
gate checks). Save the subagent's actual output to `ADVERSARY_TRANSCRIPT.md` and commit it with
the report. A verdict without its work shown is one Write away from forged; the transcript is what
the operator's independent re-run gets diffed against.

(Compute the hash: `python3 -c "import hashlib;print(hashlib.sha256(open('MONEY_LOG.md','rb').read()).hexdigest())"`)
