The Show HN Launch Playbook

Built from 14,000 Show HN posts (2026-03-14 to 2026-07-16), via the public HN Algolia API. "Front page" is proxied as 30+ points. These are correlations, not guarantees — but timing and title are free variables on launch day, so play them well.

The one-page checklist

  • Post on a weekend. Saturday (6.0%) and Sunday (5.3%) beat every weekday.
  • Aim for 16:00–20:00 UTC. Best hours: 16:00 (6.2%), 20:00 (5.8%), 17:00 (5.6%). Avoid 03:00–07:00 UTC.
  • Open with "Show HN: I built…". Personal framing over-indexes on the front page.
  • Put a concrete number in the title. Specificity ("14,000 posts", "in 300 lines") correlates with more reach.
  • Do not lead with "AI"/"LLM"/"GPT". That framing underperforms the baseline — the crowd is fatigued.
  • Keep the title tight (under ~45 chars does best) and say exactly what it is.
  • Post the first comment yourself immediately: what it is, why you built it, how it works, honest limitations. Non-promotional tone.
  • Reality check: median post gets 2 points and 53.3% get two or fewer. Timing/title move odds; they don't fix a weak product.
