If you're about to post a Show HN, the honest baseline is brutal: . Just of posts ever reach the front page (I'm using 30+ points as the proxy). So the interesting question isn't "will it blow up" — it's what separates the 4.5% from the long tail. Here's what the data says.

1. Posting hour matters about 4x

Front-page rate by the UTC hour a post was submitted. The best window is the US late-morning/early-afternoon; the dead zone is ~03:00–07:00 UTC.

2. Weekends beat weekdays

Counterintuitive, but consistent: Saturday and Sunday posts reach the front page more often than mid-week ones — likely less competition in the queue.

3. Your title: "I built", a number — yes. "AI" — no.

Front-page rate for posts whose titles match each pattern, vs the 4.5% baseline. Personal framing and a concrete number help. Leading with AI/LLM/GPT actually underperforms — that space is saturated and the crowd is fatigued.

What I'd take from this

Post Saturday or Sunday around 15:00–17:00 UTC, open with "Show HN: I built…", put a concrete number in the title, and don't lead with "AI" even if it is. None of this manufactures a good product — but on a launch you only get to fire once, the timing and title are free variables, and the data says they move the odds.
